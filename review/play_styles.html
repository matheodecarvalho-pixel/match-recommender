Play Style Atlas

Draft profiles for 140 clubs, for review before they go on the site. Updated 19 October: every club first filed as pragmatic was re-checked against its current coach and his record at previous clubs; a coach's philosophy now wins over this season's numbers. A deeper pass then went through each country's press, club announcements and coach profiles for every club below high confidence. L'Équipe, France Football and So Foot block automated reading, so French rows rely on outlets quoting them. A third pass used fan sites, local papers and coach press conferences for the clubs still at low confidence (Reddit blocks automated reading). A fourth pass read each remaining coach's Wikipedia pages, in English and in their own language. Each club has one of six styles, a confidence level, the reason, and its season averages from ESPN match stats. Click a style to filter, click a column to sort.

How the draft was made

Research: 2026-27 previews and tactical pieces (ESPN, Goal, The Athletic-style trend pieces, Get German Football News, Serie A almanacs, Al Jazeera, club coverage), summarised per club with a link. Stats: per-game averages over this season's league and European matches. Rules for clubs without research: 58%+ possession is possession; 42% or less with 15+ shots conceded is low block; 14+ fouls at 50% or less possession is direct; 3.6+ goals a game at both ends, or 15+ shots each way, is all-out attack; 16+ shots at 48%+ possession is press; everything else is pragmatic. Stats are 6 to 11 games in, so low-confidence rows will move.
