# Eyeball: brand sheet

**Name:** Eyeball (lowercase in the wordmark: "eyeball")
**Tagline:** Keep your eye on the right match.

## Logo
A classic football. The centre patch is an eye with an emerald iris; the five other patches are the flags of France (top), Spain (right), Italy (bottom right), Germany (bottom left) and England (left).

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| logo.svg / logo.png | Main logo, light backgrounds and app header |
| logo-no-flags.svg | Simpler mark when flags get too small |
| logo-reverse.svg | On dark or emerald backgrounds |
| logo-mono.svg | One colour: stamps, print |
| favicon.svg / favicon-32.png | Browser tab (no flags, stays readable at 16-32 px) |
| app-icon.png | 1024 px, home-screen icon |

## Colours
| Token | Hex | Use |

## Type (Google Fonts)

- Body / UI: **Instrument Sans** 400-600
